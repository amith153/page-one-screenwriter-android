## Data
- `app/src/main/assets/cinema` holds film titles, years, directors and short, well-known lines shown as the daily quote. The quotations remain the property of their rights holders and are included only as brief quotations. If you are a rights holder and want a line removed, open an issue.
- `app/src/main/assets/spell` is Page One's built-in word list for spell check.
- Film frames are fetched at run time from TMDB, Wikimedia Commons and Wikipedia and are not distributed with the app. If you supply a TMDB key, this product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
