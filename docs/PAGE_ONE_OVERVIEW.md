# Page One — app overview

*Version 3.0 · Android 8.0+ · status as of 19 October 2026*

Page One is a screenwriting app for Android. It writes industry-format screenplays and helps you plan the story. It also covers the production side: notes, revisions, locked pages, reports and sides. And it keeps you writing, with goals, sprints, deadlines and reminders.

---

## 1. Writing

**The editor**
- Ten element types: Scene heading, Action, Character, Parenthetical, Dialogue, Transition, Shot, Lyrics, Centered, and Note (a note to yourself that never prints).
- Smart typing:
  - Enter and Tab move to the next sensible element.
  - "int. " becomes a scene heading and "CUT TO:" becomes a transition.
  - Parentheses are added for you.
- Autocomplete for character names (including who's likely to speak next), scene locations, times of day and extensions such as (V.O.) and (O.S.).
- Automatic (CONT'D), dual dialogue, and live page breaks with page numbers.
- Spell check with a built-in 160,000-word dictionary that includes film slang, plus your personal dictionary. Character names are never flagged.
- Undo and redo, find and replace, adjustable text size.
- Focus mode (dims everything but the current line) and typewriter scrolling.
- Paper looks: white, match theme, or warm sepia.

**Views**
- **Index cards.** Every scene is a card. Drag to reorder whole scenes, colour-tag them, and filter by storyline. Cards show storyline dots and a mood thumbnail.
- **Navigator.** A scene list with search and page numbers.
- **Page view.** The script exactly as it prints, with (MORE) and (CONT'D), locked page numbers and revision colours, and a page-turn animation. Tap a line to edit it.

**Tablets, foldables and keyboards**
- On wide screens the navigator or the index cards sit beside the script.
- Keyboard shortcuts:

  | Shortcut | Action |
  |---|---|
  | Ctrl+1…0 | Set element type |
  | Shift+Tab | Previous element type |
  | Ctrl+Enter | New scene |
  | Alt+↑ / Alt+↓ | Previous / next scene |
  | Ctrl+F | Find |
  | Ctrl+D | Dual dialogue |
  | Ctrl+M | Pin a note |
  | Ctrl+K | Script Doctor |
  | Ctrl+S | Save a version |
  | Ctrl+E | Export |
  | Ctrl+B | Side panel |
  | Ctrl+Shift+P | Page view |
  | Ctrl+/ | Shortcut list |

**Script Doctor**
- A format and consistency check with 21 rules in three groups: Problems, Consistency and Polish. Examples:
  - a character cue with nothing to say
  - orphaned dialogue
  - "MAYA" and "MAYAH" spelled two ways
  - location spelling variants
  - a scene heading with no INT./EXT. or no time of day
  - a heading stranded at the bottom of a page
  - dense action paragraphs
  - a title page with no contact details
- One-tap fixes, "Tidy up N automatically", undo, and per-script ignore.
- A nudge appears before export if problems remain.

## 2. Story

- **Story bible.**
  - Character profiles: role, age, want, need, arc, voice, backstory and photo.
  - Location profiles: description, notes, photo and the scenes set there.
  - "Read only [character]'s lines" checks that a character's voice stays consistent.
- **Structure and beats.**
  - Templates: Short film, Save the Cat!, Three-act, Hero's Journey, Story Circle, one-hour TV drama, half-hour TV comedy.
  - Place scenes on beats. Page One checks where each beat lands against its target page, e.g. "Catalyst should land by p.12 — yours is on p.19".
  - A beat-map strip shows targets against your actual scenes.
  - **More** opens a plain-language guide.
- **Script X-ray.**
  - A pacing strip and a rhythm chart, coloured by INT/EXT, day/night, storyline or card colour.
  - A heatmap of which characters appear in which scene.
  - Storyline (A/B/C) management.
- **Scene sheet.** For each scene: heading, synopsis, colour, storylines, beat, a production breakdown (cast, extras, props, wardrobe, make-up, vehicles, effects, sound, set dressing, animals) and mood images.
- **Mood board.** A script-wide board plus images for each scene. Add images from the gallery or use today's film still. Tap any image for full screen (swipe between images, pinch to zoom).

---

## 3. Notes and references

- Select words in the script and tap the note button (or press Ctrl+M) to **pin a note** to them. The noted text is highlighted in the author's colour, with a marker in the margin.
- Notes support replies, resolve and reopen, and a filter for one line only. If the text changes, a note re-finds its words.
- **Attachments:** a picture, music or audio file, a video, a recorded voice memo, or a web link.
  - Files stay in your own storage. Picked files are referenced where they already are, not copied. New recordings are saved to the phone's **Recordings › Page One** folder.
  - Pictures and videos open full screen. Audio plays inside the note. Links open in the browser.

## 4. Productivity

- **Daily word goal** with a progress ring, streak, last-seven-days bars, and a "writing year" heatmap.
- **Writing sprints.** Choose a timed session (10–90 minutes) and an optional word or page goal. A live countdown runs, a bell rings at the end, and you get a summary and a sprint history.
- **Deadline planner.** Set a finish date and target length. Page One shows pages per day needed, ahead or behind your plan, and days left. It appears on the dashboard and in Statistics.
- **Idea inbox.**
  - Capture ideas from the share menu of any app ("Save idea"), the home-screen widget (Type or Record), or the inbox itself.
  - Spoken ideas are kept as audio recordings, exactly as you said them.
  - Send an idea into a script as a new card, action, dialogue or note. A voice idea arrives with its recording pinned to the line.
- **Notifications**, each with its own switch:
  - a daily writing reminder at your chosen time
  - deadline reminders (daily, plus countdowns at 2 weeks, 1 week, 3 days and 1 day, due today, and overdue)
  - streak at risk
  - the sprint bell
  - a weekly idea-inbox nudge
  - goal celebrations
- **Home-screen widgets:** Idea inbox, and Writing goal (with today's ring, streak and quote).
- **Daily inspiration card:** a film still and a famous line, different every day, also available offline.

---

## 5. Production

- **Revisions.** Revision mode with colour sets (Blue, Pink, Yellow…) and dates, asterisks in the margin, and coloured pages in the PDF.
- **Versions.** Auto-saved and named snapshots, with restore.
- **Production lock.**
  - Freezes scene numbers and page breaks.
  - New scenes get A-numbers (12A, 12B), overflowing pages become A-pages (34A), and deleted scenes stay as OMITTED.
- **Reports** as PDF or CSV: scenes (with length in eighths of a page), characters, locations, and breakdown sheets.
- **Sides.** Choose characters to get a PDF of only their scenes, with the script's scene numbers.
- **Statistics.** Pages, scenes, words, dialogue vs action, INT/EXT and day/night balance, character speaking time, and the longest scenes.

---

## 6. Export, sharing and collaboration

- **Export formats:** PDF (industry format in Courier Prime, US Letter or A4, scene numbers, title page, watermark), Final Draft (.fdx) and Fountain. Kannada, Hindi and other Indic scripts print cleanly with a taller line.
- **Annotated PDF.** "Include notes & references" colour-marks the noted text, with a dot in the margin.
- **Import:** Fountain, Final Draft and .pageone files, from the app or the share menu.
- **Live co-writing.** Real-time editing with join codes or invite links, co-writer cursors, "follow" mode, and a change history you can revert. The story bible, beats, storylines, breakdown, lock, deadline and notes are shared too; images stay on the phone that added them.
- **Merge.** Send a .pageone file to a co-writer and merge it back. Conflicts are marked with notes.
- **Backup.**
  - Google Drive: automatic backup and restore of your scripts.
  - Kept-in-sync copies in Dropbox, OneDrive or any storage app.

## 7. Look and feel

- **Themes:** System, Light, Dark and Pure black, with layered "studio" surfaces in dark modes and warm ivory in light.
- **Twelve accents:** Champagne, Pewter, Taupe, Terracotta, Blush, Bordeaux, Mauve, Slate, Navy, Sage, Emerald and Bronze. Material You wallpaper colours are optional.
- Dashboard: a soft accent glow, cards with a hairline gradient edge, gradient progress rings, and a streak flame in the theme gradient.
- An ink-and-gold app icon and splash screen.
- A four-slide welcome tour on first install, with goal and reminder setup and an optional sample screenplay (*The Last Projectionist*).

## 8. Under the hood (for the developer)

- **Code:** Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, Material 3. The core logic (formatting, pagination, Script Doctor, structure, production lock, reports, planner) is plain Kotlin with 30 unit tests.
- **Storage:** one JSON file per script, with versions and a library index. Story data lives in the same file.
- **Services:** Firebase Firestore for live co-writing, the Google Drive REST API with the narrow `drive.file` permission, and PdfBox-Android for PDF links.
- **Builds:** debug and release are signed with the same key (`pageone-release.jks`). `build-share-apk.bat` produces `PageOne-share.apk`.
